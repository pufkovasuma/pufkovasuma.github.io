Privacy Policy — Pufkova Šuma

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Pufkova Šuma uses its own tool, "Pufkov svijet upload", only to upload and schedule its own videos to the Pufkova Šuma channel accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

  • The tool does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or any other users.
  • We do not collect any data from children.
  • Access tokens for our own accounts are stored only locally on the channel owner's computer and are used only to publish videos.
  • The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms); see also the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Access can be revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions and data deletion requests: pufkovasuma@gmail.com
